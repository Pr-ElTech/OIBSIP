About Dickson Nsofor

Dickson Nsofor is a Nigerian technology entrepreneur, investor, and business leader best known as the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Kora.

His passion for innovation and financial technology has enabled businesses across Africa to access world-class payment infrastructure through a unified platform.

His leadership reflects a commitment to solving meaningful problems, supporting entrepreneurs, and building technology capable of connecting Africa to the global economy.
